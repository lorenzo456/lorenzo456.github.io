Hi, and welcome! For a while now I've wanted a place to share things that don't quite fit into a paper or a lecture slide, so here it is.

If you've found your way here, you might already know a bit about me from the [home page](/index.html). In short: I'm an Assistant Professor in Information Systems at IÉSEG School of Management in Lille, and my research looks at how games and AI can support people's health.

## What I'll write about

- **Research.** The ideas behind my work on AI and games for mental health, explained without the academic jargon, plus behind-the-scenes stories from projects and conferences. You can find the papers themselves on my [Research page](/Research.html).
- **Games and AI.** Things I find interesting where the two meet, an area that has fascinated me ever since I read *Artificial Intelligence and Games* by Georgios Yannakakis and Julian Togelius.
- **Teaching.** Notes and tips from teaching programming and data management.
- **Side projects.** Progress on the retro games I build in my spare time, which I hope to release one day.

## Say hello

If something here sparks an idea, a question, or a possible collaboration, I'd love to hear from you. The [Contact page](/Contact.html) has the best ways to reach me.

Thanks for reading, and see you in the next post!
