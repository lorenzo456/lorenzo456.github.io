Lorenzo James

Games AI Information Systems

I'm an Assistant Professor in Management of Information Systems at IÉSEG School of Management. I did my PhD at Eindhoven University of Technology, where I researched how AI can personalize gamified mobile health apps, from LLM-generated treatment goals to automatically planned game content. My background is in game development, and games are still at the heart of how I think about getting people engaged with technology.
